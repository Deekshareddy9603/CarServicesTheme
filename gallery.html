Precision Car Wash Services

Understanding our cleanroom-grade washing techniques engineered to safeguard your paint finish while restoring showroom clarity.

pH-Neutral Snow Foam

High-pressure cannons blanket the vehicle in dense foam, encapsulating grit and road grime to dissolve dirt touchlessly without scratching the clear coat.

  • Prevents swirl marks & micro-scratches
  • 100% Safe for ceramic coatings

Alloy & Caliper Decon

Iron-dissolving chemical agents lift metallic brake dust and road tar from rim barrels and calipers, followed by soft-bristle detailing and high-temp sealant.

  • Removes corrosive embedded brake dust
  • Hydrophobic shield against future grime

Deep Interior Steam

Superheated 140°C dry steam sanitizes AC vents, upholstery, and leather seams, killing 99.9% of bacteria and allergens without harsh chemical residues.

  • Chemical-free deep purification
  • Restores natural leather oils & matte look

Paint Correction Polish

Dual-action rotary buffers level micro-scratches, oxidation, and haze using precision micro-abrasive compounds to reveal optical paint mirror depth.

  • Eliminates up to 90% of spiderweb swirls
  • Restores deep color reflections

Nano-Ceramic 9H Shield

A hand-applied silica glass polymer fuses directly with factory clear coat, providing 3+ years of protection against UV rays, acid rain, and bird drops.

  • Extreme hydrophobic water beading
  • Effortless self-cleaning properties

Engine Bay Vapor Clean

Low-moisture vapor degreasing clears heavy oil and dust from engine compartments safely without wetting sensitive electronic wiring and sensors.

  • Safe for modern electrical components
  • Anti-static dress prevents dust buildup
